        longest june is my little hub on the internet: a place for the projects I'm working on and the things I'm slowly building.

        The name comes from the summer solstice, the longest day of the year. That's the feeling I want everything here to have: unhurried, warm, with a little more light than you expected.

        Some of what lives here is finished. Most of it is still in progress, and that's on purpose. I'd rather show the work while it's happening.
